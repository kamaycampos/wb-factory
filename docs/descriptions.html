“Why your mom yelled at you”
KT_TAPWATER/01_WHY-YOUR-MOM-YELLED-AT-YOU_99s.mp4
Kevin Trudeau tells a story about being a kid who did dumb things: eating dirt, chewing on a styrofoam cup.

His mother yelled every single time, hard enough that one day he cried and told her, "You just like being mean."

She said it would be a lot easier on her if she didn't care.

Years later, a friend's mother never once raised her voice. That friend cried too, for the opposite reason: "My mother doesn't care about me."

The lesson Kevin took from it: the parent correcting you is the one paying attention.

One drank a gallon of milk a day. One smoked unfiltered cigarettes. One was climbing trees in the Amazon in his 90s.

The only pattern: food grown locally and eaten in season, and fermented food the rest of the year. Not one of them was taking supplements.

So he decided to just eat real food, and fill the gaps with whole food.
